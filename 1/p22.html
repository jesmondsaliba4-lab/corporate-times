Election Watch · Europe & the world

The votes that will shape 2027

From Madrid on 29 November to Buenos Aires next October: the elections on Europe’s calendar and the world’s, and what each one decides.

First up · Sunday 29 November
Spain

Pedro Sánchez called the snap general election on 5 October, after Congress rejected two of his government’s housing decrees. Congress has 350 seats; 176 make a majority.

Alberto Núñez Feijóo’s Partido Popular leads in most private polls, with Santiago Abascal’s Vox third; the state pollster CIS puts Sánchez’s PSOE first. Several private polls project the PP and Vox together above 176 seats; the CIS does not.

Then · by late December
Serbia elects a president

On 25 October the Student List — formed out of the protests that followed the Novi Sad station canopy collapse in November 2024 — won 140 of the National Assembly’s 250 seats with 53.7% of the vote. The list led by the Serbian Progressive Party (SNS), “Aleksandar Vučić – United Serbia”, took 103 seats with 39.5%.

Vučić had resigned the presidency on 27 September to run for prime minister. The new Speaker, Aleksandar Čupić, said on 20 November that the presidential election would be called on 27 November; it must be held by late December.

In Brussels, the European People’s Party leadership moved on 18 November to suspend the SNS, a decision its Political Assembly is due to ratify on 7–8 December.
