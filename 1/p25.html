Poland
Sejm and Senate · autumn 2027

The first parliamentary vote since Karol Nawrocki became president in 2025. His vetoes have constrained Donald Tusk’s coalition; the election decides whether that cohabitation continues. The two Confederation parties, polled separately, together match the main opposition party, Law and Justice (PiS), on the trend.

Finland
Parliament · 18 April 2027

The first parliamentary election since Finland joined NATO in April 2023. The opposition Social Democrats lead; on current numbers Prime Minister Petteri Orpo’s four-party coalition (National Coalition, Finns, Swedish People’s Party, Christian Democrats) would hold 78 of the 200 seats.
