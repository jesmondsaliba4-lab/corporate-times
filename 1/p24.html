Four European votes to watch

France
President · 18 April, run-off 2 May

Emmanuel Macron cannot stand again. Marine Le Pen of the National Rally (RN) declared on 7 July, hours after the Paris Court of Appeal upheld her embezzlement conviction but reduced her ban from office, leaving her eligible in principle; she is appealing to the Cour de Cassation. She has said Jordan Bardella would be her prime minister. The centre is split between Édouard Philippe and Gabriel Attal; Raphaël Glucksmann carries Socialist backing.

Italy
Parliament · by 22 December 2027

Parliament gave final approval on 8 October to Giorgia Meloni’s electoral reform. It replaces the mixed system in place since 2017 with proportional representation, gives a party or coalition that clears 42% a bonus of up to 70 seats in the 400-seat Chamber and 35 in the 200-seat Senate, and requires coalitions to name their candidate for prime minister. Opponents call the bonus disproportionate; supporters say it will deliver stable majorities.
