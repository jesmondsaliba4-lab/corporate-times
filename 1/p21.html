China · Russia · US
Three powers, one table
Xi Jinping, Vladimir Putin and Donald Trump held the first trilateral meeting of their three countries in China on 19 November. Their joint statement committed them to “strategic stability”, said that “nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought”, promised more refined petroleum products — and declared that the International Criminal Court “must not abuse” its jurisdiction. Xi called the meeting “groundbreaking”.

Climate
The hottest year, almost certainly
As COP31 opened in Turkey, Berkeley Earth said it was 95% confident that 2026 would beat 2024 as the hottest year on record. Copernicus confirmed that October was the hottest October measured. “We have had a run of record warm years,” said Dr Robert Rohde.

Health
A pill against the hardest cancer
In an early trial, cancer became undetectable on scans in one in 12 patients given daraxonrasib with chemotherapy, and tumours stopped growing in almost all of the 61 participants with advanced pancreatic cancer. Cancer Research UK called it “the biggest leap forward in decades”; doctors stress it is not a cure.
