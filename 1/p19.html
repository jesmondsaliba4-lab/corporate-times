Europe’s widening circle

While Britain weighs the way back, others are queuing at the door — or building new ones.

Canada
The first associate member?
Mark Carney wants talks on Canada becoming the EU’s first associate member concluded by June 2027. The status has no precedent in the treaties, and Germany objected to the term during the summit’s declaration, which committed instead to an “ever closer association”.
Comuniq.EU · 2 Nov
Montenegro
The 28th member, in 2028?
For the first time since Croatia in 2011, the Commission says it can soon close accession talks with a candidate — Montenegro, population 630,000 — possibly by the end of 2026, with a treaty signed as soon as June 2027.
Comuniq.EU · 5 Nov
Serbia
A standing ovation and a list of conditions
A month after the election that ended Aleksandar Vučić’s nine years in power, the European People’s Party moved to suspend his SNS. Its Political Assembly decides on 7–8 December.
Comuniq.EU · 19 Nov
Brussels
Everyone wants to work for the EU
174,727 people applied for 1,490 places in EPSO’s biggest-ever generalist competition, almost eight times the 2019 figure. Italians were 45.4% of them — more than Spain, Germany and France combined.
Comuniq.EU · 10 Nov
Budget
A €2 trillion argument
Parliament opened its debate on the 2028–34 budget, proposed at almost €2tn, about 1.26% of gross national income. Austria wants deeper cuts, and leaders must agree unanimously on a €45bn loan to Ukraine.
Comuniq.EU · 20 Nov
Ukraine
Kallas: Russia’s demands are “absurd”
The EU’s foreign policy chief rejected Moscow’s peace demands as the Commission readied a €45bn loan, guaranteed — like the earlier €90bn — by headroom in the EU budget.
