The Europeans who were eight when Britain voted to leave

The summit of 20 November gives the Brexit debate something it has often lacked: specific decisions against which people can judge the value and the price of closer ties.

Essay · Jesmond Saliba · 21 November 2026

A child who was eight when Britain voted to leave the European Union is eighteen today. For a young person in Malta considering a British university, the most consequential words to come out of yesterday’s summit in Brussels may be the six months London and Brussels have given themselves to agree on tuition fees. A place at university can be offered, a language shared and a family connection strong. The figure on the offer letter can still decide where that young person spends the next three years.
