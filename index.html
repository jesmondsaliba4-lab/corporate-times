Co-owned media

A publication that belongs to your world, run by a newsroom that knows how.

An industry, a profession or a company has its own news, its own facts and its own conversations. We give them a title of their own, and the editorial craft to keep it credible, edition after edition.

01

You co-own the title

The masthead carries the name your readers know. You share in the title’s ownership and its direction, and you help shape the audience it builds.

02

We run the newsroom

Our editors research, write, translate, lay out and publish every edition, in print and online, to the rhythm your readers need.

03

Readers can trust it

Every item is written in our own words and names its source. Editorial and advertising are kept apart, and an approvals desk lets your editors sign off what goes out.
